# Surface brief: Home

## Scope
Route: `/` (home). Public Persuade surface. Redesign (replacement world). Inherits to all public pages (About, Services, Portfolio, Contact) via the world; each keeps its own brief if needed.

## Mode
Persuade.

## Audience & job
Indonesian business owner evaluating a digital partner for a website, app, or content strategy. Job: decide whether Nusantara Digital is credible and transparent enough to contact. Must know what this is, why it matters, and what to do within seconds.

## Constraints
- All copy in Bahasa Indonesia (English only as section eyebrows).
- Content comes from Laravel API; do not hardcode services/portfolios/pages.
- No fabricated proof: stats (120+, 45, 8) are unverified; mark or remove.
- No testimonials, client logos, or verified metrics on hand.

## Chosen direction
Label Stock (product packaging world). Assigned by roll (index 4 of 7).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The company profile is a product package — a label you can read, a barcode you can scan, a box you can open — refusing the category default of a gradient hero over a three-card grid. Transparency is the mechanism, and a package's label is the most honest surface in commerce: contents, weight, batch, instructions, and a customer-service line, all printed where you can see them.

OWN-WORLD: Kraft-cream ground (#f2ead9) with warm near-black ink (#1c1a16), a stamp-red primary accent (#b23a2a) carrying CTAs and seals, and a foil-green secondary (#2a5d3a) for badges. Material: kraft board, label stock, ink, foil stamp, ruled lines, barcode. Type: Archivo Black for product-name headlines, Work Sans for body, JetBrains Mono for SKU/batch/labels. Corner language: square label corners with occasional die-cut notch; hairline rules; no glass, no gradients, no rounded SaaS cards.

STORY: The visitor understands within one viewport that this is a studio that prints its contents on the label — services, process, portfolio, price posture — and believes transparency is a practice, not a slogan. They do one thing: open the box (contact). The path runs label → contents → how to use → batch (portfolio) → customer service (contact).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed kraft-cream label. Top: a label header bar with the ND monogram, company name as product name in Archivo Black, and a SKU/batch line in mono. Center-left: the offer as the product descriptor in large type, with a 'contents' list of services as labeled rows. Center-right: a barcode block with a serial and a 'net weight / scope' line. Bottom: a stamp-red 'Hubungi customer service' seal as the primary action, plus a foil-green 'terakreditasi' badge. One dominant move: the product name at label scale; everything else holds still in label material at real reading size.
